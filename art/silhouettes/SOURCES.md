# Silhouettes: sources and licenses

100 solid silhouettes, each scaled to fit a 340px box centered on a 400×400 canvas, fill `#222`.
All come from openly licensed icon sets that allow commercial use. Sets marked CC BY require a credit line.

## Credit line (required for the CC BY sets)

> Icons from Game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0, by Lorc, Delapouite and contributors) and Font Awesome Free (CC BY 4.0). Additional icons from Material Design Icons and IconPark (Apache 2.0) and Pinhead (CC0).

## Sets

- **Game-icons.net**: CC BY 3.0, https://game-icons.net
- **Font Awesome Free**: CC BY 4.0, https://fontawesome.com

- **Pinhead**: CC0, https://pinhead.ink
